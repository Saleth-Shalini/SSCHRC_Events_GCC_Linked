[36] Background:
[37] Global Cancer Consortium
[38] Global Cancer Consortium: established in University of Kentucky, USA, GCC has chapters across the globe: South Asia Chapter, North America Chapter, East Asia Chapter, Europe Chapter, West and Africa Chapter. Empowered professional partnerships of innovators and leaders who collaborate to execute new ideas, plan events and guide the overall direction of GCC.  Global cancer consortium has the mission to foster trans-disciplinary collaborations through education, research, treatment and community outreach overcome cancer. GCC empowers professional partnerships that enhance cancer care and provide mentored training of next generation of scientists and clinicians.
[39] Over the past five years, GCC have gathered the most brilliant minds and leading institutions from around the world to collaborate in the fight against cancer, through research, continued education and mentoring the next generation towards leading the way in cancer care.
[40] Profile of SSCHRC:
[41] Sri Shankara Cancer Foundation:
[42] Sri Shankara Cancer Foundation (SSCF) is a not-for-profit organization, registered under Section 12A. Sri Shankara Cancer Foundation was conceived in 2008, by a group of forward-thinking individuals, under the visionary leadership of Managing Trustee, Dr. B.S. Srinath, a seasoned professional with 50 years of experience in surgical oncology.
[43] SSCF was established to provide inclusive cancer treatment to each and every patient, with a specific emphasis on providing the best cancer treatment regardless of their socio-economic background. Sri Shankara Cancer Foundation is committed to alleviating the physical, emotional, and financial burdens faced by cancer patients and their families, while also addressing the broader societal impact of this disease.
[44] Sri Shankara Cancer Hospital and Research Centre, Bangalore & Bagchi Sri Shankara Cancer Centre and Research Institute, Bhubaneswar are the epicenters of comprehensive and transformative oncology. It is the nucleus where cutting-edge technology and compassionate expertise come together to deliver the best cancer treatment and care.
[45] Within our distinct departments, experts equipped with state-of-the-art facilities focus on a specialized aspect of care, ensuring personalized and top-tier treatment for cancer patients
[46] Explore our integrated specialties covering cancer prevention, screening, diagnosis, treatment, and support, all thoughtfully curated under one roof.
[47] Our mission extends to the implementation of effective cancer prevention strategies and spearheading cutting-edge research in oncology through our daughter institutions -áSri Shankara National Centre for Cancer Prevention & Researcháand the training of healthcare personnel for optimal patient care through our association with National Board of Examinations , Super specialty Fellowship Programmes under Sri Shankara College of Nursingá, áSri Shankara Institute of Allied Health Sciences under Rajiv Gandhi University of Health sciences.
[48] Sri Shankara Cancer Hospital and Research Centre joined Global Cancer Consortium in 2025. Meeting of GCC and SSCHRC in April 2026 has paved way for hosting the 8th International Conference on 29 and 30 January 2027.Organ specific cancers will be the focus of deliberations in this International Conference. It was strongly felt participation of Clinicians and Molecular Scientists will promote and enhance research collaboration as well strengthen relationships to find solutions to various facets of cancer care.
[49] Objectives of the Conference:
[50] To evolve opportunities for Clinicians and Molecular Scientists to come together and advance knowledge on Cancer.
[51] To provide opportunities for post graduate students,  PhD scholars to acquire  advanced knowledge in oncology interacting with members of Global Cancer Consortium .
[52] To promote research collaboration between institutions and individuals involved in Cancer care from across the Globe.
[53] To promote mutual exchange of information between Clinicians, Scientists, Technologists, Students, Faculty from across the Globe.
