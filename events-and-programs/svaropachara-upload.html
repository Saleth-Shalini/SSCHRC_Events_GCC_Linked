Upload Svaropachara Flyer

Add a flyer to Upcoming Events or Completed Events. It will appear automatically on the Svaropachara page.

How to use: Fill the form, choose the flyer image/PDF, enter the upload password, then submit. Default password: sschrc-svaropachara-2026 (change it in svaropachara-upload.php before going live).

This page needs a PHP server (XAMPP / live hosting). On plain python -m http.server, upload will not work — use manual method below.

Manual upload (no PHP)

  1. Copy your flyer into assets/svaropachara/flyers/upcoming/ or .../completed/
  2. Open svaropachara-events.json and add a new entry under upcoming or completed
  3. Refresh svaropachara.html
