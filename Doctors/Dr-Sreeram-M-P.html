Professional Overview

Dr. Sreeram M. P is an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon with over 10 years of experience in the management of head and neck cancers. His clinical expertise includes oral cancer, salivary gland malignancies, premalignant oral lesions, oropharyngeal cancers, neck swellings, access osteotomies, and osteoradionecrosis.

He serves as the Deputy Co-Chair of the Translational Research Committee of the Head and Neck Cancer International Group (HNCIG) and is a Research Committee Member of the Salivary Gland Cancer Research Group (UK) and the International Society of Oral Oncology (ISOO). He is actively involved in international research collaborations, including work with the International Centre of Recurrent Cancer (IReC) led by The Royal Marsden Hospital, London.

Dr. Sreeram has contributed as a Co-Principal Investigator in Phase III clinical trials and has authored over 25 peer-reviewed publications and two textbooks (Springer). He is affiliated with major professional bodies including IHNSG, HNCIG, ISOO, MASCC, IAOO, AOCMF, FHNO, and AOMSI.

His primary research focus is oral cancer translational research
