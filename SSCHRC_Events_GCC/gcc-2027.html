Overview

About the Conference

We are delighted to welcome you to participate in the 8th International Conference of the Global Cancer Consortium scheduled to be held in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India with the theme “Molecular Approaches to Clinical Oncology Multidisciplinary approaches, Innovative Science and Technologies.”

The conference is Jointly organized by Sri Shankara Cancer Hospital & Research Centre and GCC South Asia Chapter.

This event provides an excellent opportunity for experts in this field of Oncology, both Clinicians and Molecular Scientists, to share their knowledge, experience and network with the global cancer community, gain visibility, contribute to finding solutions to cancer control and methods, and advance translational research. This conference offers opportunities for clinicians and molecular scientists, including students and trainees, to share their research achievements through oral and poster presentations.

Scope

Focus

Breast Ovary Pancreas Colon Urinary Bladder Lung

Malignancy related to Breast, Ovary, Pancreas, Colon, Urinary Bladder, and Lung will be the major focus. It is expected that Clinicians, Scientists, Postgraduate and PhD Scholars representing national and international academic institutions, research centers, hospitals, pharma and other cancer-related industrial sectors will participate in-person as well as virtually.

Oncologists Molecular Scientists Residents Scholars Academicians Administrators

Oncologists, Molecular Scientists, Oncology super-specialty course residents, scholars, Fellowship Scholars, Academicians, Administrators, and other stakeholders from across the country and five chapters of GCC across the Globe.

Background

Global Cancer Consortium

The Global Cancer Consortium (GCC) was established in 2020 by the Markey Cancer Center with Mayo Clinic in USA and Tata Memorial Centre, MAHE, and Saroj Gupta Cancer Centre and Research Institute in India. GCC now has chapters across the globe, including South Asia Chapter, North America Chapter, East Asia Chapter, Europe Chapter, and Africa Chapter. The non-profit GCC has the mission to foster trans-disciplinary collaborations to overcome cancer through education, research, treatment and community outreach. To accomplish these objectives, GCC empowers professional partnerships that enhance cancer research, education and healthcare (www.globalcancerconsortia.org).

Over the past five years, GCC has gathered the most brilliant minds and leading institutions from around the world to collaborate in the fight against cancer, through research, continued education and mentoring the next generation towards leading the way in cancer care.

Host Institution

Sri Shankara Cancer Hospital & Research Centre

Sri Shankara Cancer Hospital and Research Centre (SSCHRC), Bengaluru, is a 520-bed comprehensive cancer centre and the flagship unit of Sri Shankara Cancer Foundation (SSCF), NABH- and NABL-accredited, that has treated over 1,20,000 new cancer patients since 2012.

Read more about SSCHRC Read less

Research here is institutionally embedded. SSCHRC and its academic arm, Sri Shankara National Centre for Cancer Prevention and Research (SSNCCPR), are recognized by India’s Department of Scientific and Industrial Research as a Scientific and Industrial Research Organization (DSIR-SIRO). A dedicated Department of Molecular Diagnostics and Research runs an active translational program across multiple cancer types, anchored by an in-house core genomics facility generating molecular data directly from clinical cases. The program has produced 200+ peer-reviewed publications, multiple patents, and an active multi-cancer biobank supporting ongoing translational studies, built on collaboration with leading national and international research institutions and industry partners.

Beyond hospital-based care, SSCHRC’s Community Oncology Department extends the institution’s reach into the population it serves, running sustained awareness campaigns on cancer risk and early symptoms alongside regular screening camps in underserved communities. This outreach identifies cases earlier, when treatment outcomes are strongest, and reflects SSCHRC’s broader commitment to reducing the cancer burden beyond the walls of the hospital itself. SSCHRC also serves as a training institute, running postgraduate, super-speciality DNB, DrNB, and fellowship programmes, and PhD programmes since 2024, in affiliation with national medical education bodies and universities - National Board of Examinations, Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Science (RGUHS), Manipal Academy of Higher Education.

In early 2025, SSCF extended this platform eastward with a second 750-bed hospital in Bhubaneswar, expanding the patient and bio specimen base available for translational research.

SSCHRC joined the Global Cancer Consortium (GCC) in 2025. Following a GCC-SSCHRC meeting in April 2026, SSCHRC will host the 8th International Conference on 29-30 January 2027, focused on organ-specific cancers, aimed at deepening research collaboration between clinicians and molecular scientists across member institutions.

Goals

Objectives of the Conference

  • To evolve opportunities for Clinicians and Molecular Scientists to come together and advance knowledge on Cancer.
  • To provide opportunities for post graduate students, PhD scholars to acquire advanced knowledge in oncology interacting with members of Global Cancer Consortium.
  • To promote research collaboration between institutions and individuals involved in Cancer care from across the Globe.
  • To promote mutual exchange of information between Clinicians, Scientists, Technologists, Students, Faculty from across the Globe.
